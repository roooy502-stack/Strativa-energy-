Privacy policy

How this website handles personal information. Last updated 19 November 2026.

About this policy

This policy describes how Strativa Energy handles personal information collected through strativaenergy.com.au. You can contact us at info@strativaenergy.com.au with any question about it.

Information we collect

  • Enquiry form. If you use the enquiry form, we receive the details you enter: your name, company, email address, phone number, project type and stage, and your message.
  • Email. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to send.
  • Website usage. We use Google Analytics and Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand how the website is used. These services may use cookies or similar identifiers and collect technical information such as pages visited, approximate location, browser and device type.

How we use it

We use enquiry and email details to respond to you and discuss your project. We use analytics information to understand and improve the website.

Service providers

Enquiry form submissions are delivered to us by FormSubmit, a third-party form service. Analytics are provided by Google and Cloudflare. Some of these providers may process information outside Australia. We do not sell personal information.

Access, correction and complaints

You can ask to access or correct the personal information we hold about you by emailing us. If you are not satisfied with how a privacy concern is handled, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last updated.
