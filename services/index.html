Development services for Australian solar and BESS projects

Strativa Energy provides owner-side support across the workstreams that determine whether a utility-scale solar or battery energy storage opportunity can progress in Australia.

Six services, one development function

Our services are combined and sequenced to suit the project stage, internal capability and investment priorities of each developer. Four of them have their own pages with more detail; market entry and workstream management are described below.

Market Entry Framework

Orient, establish and execute. We begin with a market landscape review, regulatory mapping, stakeholder identification and an initial risk assessment. We then support consultant procurement, study sequencing and governance and reporting setup, before moving into active development coordination, approvals lodgement and stakeholder engagement.

How services fit the project stages

Engagements follow the project forward in four stages: explore, establish, connect and deliver. The services above are drawn on as each stage requires.

Explore

Market orientation, site screening and early diligence. Establishes the entry roadmap, priority opportunities and initial risk position.

Establish

Land, stakeholder, planning and environmental workstreams. Moves a priority site from concept to an approval-ready development program.

Connect

Grid strategy, applications, studies and registration. Coordinates the pathway from connection enquiry through offer and performance standards.

Delivery

Integrated diligence, risk management and FID readiness. Gives investors and buyers a clear, decision-ready view of project quality and residual risk.
