From entry roadmap to FID readiness.

See how Strativa Energy sequences Australian renewable energy development support from market exploration through connection and FID readiness.

Services are combined and sequenced to suit the project stage, internal capability and investment priorities.

01

Explore

Market orientation, site screening and early diligence. Establishes the entry roadmap, priority opportunities and initial risk position.

02

Establish

Land, stakeholder, planning and environmental workstreams. Moves a priority site from concept to an approval-ready development program.

03

Connect

Grid strategy, applications, studies and registration. Coordinates the pathway from connection enquiry through offer and performance standards.

04

Delivery

Integrated diligence, risk management and FID readiness. Gives investors and buyers a clear, decision-ready view of project quality and residual risk.

A clear engagement model

Each engagement has an agreed scope, defined deliverables, regular governance meetings and a clear reporting framework.
