About Strativa Energy

An owner-side development function for international developer teams entering and progressing utility-scale solar and battery energy storage projects in Australia.

Who we are

Strativa Energy provides early-stage development support for utility-scale solar and battery energy storage projects in Australia, acting as an owner-side development function for international developer teams.

Our capability is focused on the workstreams that determine whether an opportunity can progress: market entry, planning and environmental approvals, cultural heritage, land and stakeholders, grid connection, governance and due diligence.

We help international developer teams establish an effective Australian development presence without building a full local organisation too early. Structured workstreams, clear reporting and decision-ready advice give head office reliable visibility of project progress, risk and priorities.

Where we are based

Strativa Energy is based in Melbourne, Victoria, and supports projects in Australia. Our contact page has our email address and enquiry form.

Why local capability matters

Australian projects face layers of federal and state regulation, each with its own triggers and timelines, and every jurisdiction has distinct planning and permitting requirements. Cultural heritage and environmental assessment add scope, cost and program risk. Grid access needs technical depth and correct sequencing, and council, landowner and network relationships require consistent local presence.

Our services are built around those pressure points, so that one accountable interface sits across approvals, land, stakeholders, grid, advisers and governance.

Strativa's role

We act as the single point of coordination between your investment strategy and local project delivery. We help you test the market, establish the right development pathway and coordinate local delivery, with clear governance and decision-ready reporting.

  • Translate strategy into an Australian execution plan
  • Integrate development workstreams
  • Procure and manage specialist advisers
  • Coordinate local interfaces
  • Provide clear governance and reporting

How we work with developers

Our delivery model is designed for international developer teams entering a new regulatory and commercial environment. Advice, procurement and coordination stay focused on the developer's objectives and investment interests, and Australian capability can scale as the portfolio matures.

Each engagement has an agreed scope, defined deliverables, regular governance meetings and a clear reporting framework. Read more about what owner-side development support involves.
