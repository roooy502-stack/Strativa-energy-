What is owner-side development support for Australian solar and BESS projects?

A short explanation of what an owner-side development function does and why international developers use one.

By Strativa Energy · Published 19 November 2026

The short answer

Owner-side development support is an Australian development function that works for the project owner, typically an international developer, and focuses on the owner's objectives and investment interests. At Strativa Energy it covers market entry, planning and environmental approvals, land and stakeholder liaison, grid connection, governance and due diligence.

Why international developers need local capability

Australian projects face layers of federal and state regulation, each with its own triggers and timelines, and every jurisdiction has distinct planning and permitting requirements. Cultural heritage and environmental assessment add scope, cost and program risk, grid access needs technical depth and correct sequencing, and council, landowner and network relationships require consistent local presence.

An owner-side function lets a developer establish an Australian development presence without building a full local organisation too early, and scale that capability as the portfolio matures.

What the support covers

Strativa Energy organises its support into six services:

  • Market Entry Framework
  • Development Workstream Management
  • Planning & Environmental Approvals
  • Land & Stakeholder Liaison
  • Grid & Connection Approvals
  • Due Diligence

How engagements are structured

Each engagement has an agreed scope, defined deliverables, regular governance meetings and a clear reporting framework. Work follows the project through four stages: explore, establish, connect and deliver. Services are combined and sequenced to suit the project stage, the developer's internal capability and its investment priorities.
