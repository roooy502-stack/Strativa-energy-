Planning and environmental approvals for solar and BESS projects

A clear, defensible approvals pathway for utility-scale solar and battery energy storage projects, coordinated on the developer's behalf.

What this service covers

  • State and local planning pathways
  • Development applications and planning amendments
  • Cultural Heritage Management Plans and Registered Aboriginal Party engagement
  • Ecological studies
  • EPBC Act and state referrals
  • Local-authority liaison

Why approvals need early attention

Australian projects face layers of federal and state regulation, each with its own triggers and timelines, and every jurisdiction has distinct planning and permitting requirements. Cultural heritage and environmental assessment add scope, cost and program risk, so the pathway and the order of studies are worth settling early.

Strativa Energy acts as the owner-side coordinator, procuring and managing the specialist advisers involved and keeping head office informed with clear, decision-ready reporting.

Where it fits in an engagement

Planning and environmental work sits mainly in the Establish stage, where land, stakeholder, planning and environmental workstreams move a priority site from concept to an approval-ready development program. It runs alongside land and stakeholder liaison and ahead of grid connection applications.
