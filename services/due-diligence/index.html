Due diligence for Australian solar and BESS projects

An independent view of project quality and risk before capital is committed.

What this service covers

  • Review of yield, constraints and buildability
  • Permits and obligations
  • Tenure, encumbrances and access rights
  • Connection status, cost and curtailment exposure
  • Red flags, mitigations and go/no-go advice

Why independence matters

Strativa Energy works on the owner side. Our advice is focused on the developer's objectives and investment interests, which gives investors and buyers a clear, decision-ready view of project quality and residual risk.

Where it fits in an engagement

Early diligence sits in the Explore stage, alongside market orientation and site screening, and helps set the initial risk position. Integrated diligence and risk management return in the Delivery stage as the project moves towards FID readiness. Findings draw on the approvals, land and grid workstreams.
